This notice explains how Foodie Explorerz handles information you choose to share with us through this website or when you contact us.

01 / INFORMATION WE RECEIVE

What you
share with us.

If you contact us by email, Instagram or another channel, you may provide details such as your name, business or brand name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and information about your enquiry or project. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

02 / HOW WE USE IT

Only to respond
and communicate.

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, understand your business requirements, discuss potential projects, provide requested information and communicate about relevant services or collaborations.

03 / SHARING

Limited to
what is needed.

We do not sell your personal information. Our website enquiry form is submitted through Formspree, which processes the details you provide so your enquiry can be delivered to us. Other service providers may also handle information as needed to support website operations or communications. We do not otherwise share enquiry details with third parties except where required by law or with your consent.

04 / SECURITY & RETENTION

Handled with
care.

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to deal with the enquiry, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable legal requirements. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05 / YOUR REQUESTS

Questions or
corrections.

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have provided or to request access to or correction of it, subject to applicable law. Contact foodieexplorerz@gmail.com.
