OUR STORY / SINCE 2021

Rooted in food.
Built for growth.

Foodie Explorerz began with a simple belief: discovering a place to eat should feel easy, exciting and welcoming.

Built from first-hand exploration of Hong Kong's dining scene, our flagship food media platform grew into a community of 26.7K+ engaged local foodies. Years spent discovering restaurants and speaking with diners have given us an insider's perspective on the details that matter — from cuisine and neighbourhood to dietary needs and trust.

That audience understanding now powers our work with restaurants, hospitality groups, food and beverage product brands, retailers and online stores. We combine brand strategy, content, curated creator partnerships, search discovery and conversion systems to help good venues reach the right people and make it easier for them to visit.
